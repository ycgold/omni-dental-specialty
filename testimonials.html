Your Privacy

Your Privacy and Patient Stories

We do not share health information on this page. The U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) says the Privacy Rule, a federal law, gives you rights over your health information and sets rules and limits on who can look at and receive it.

  1. 01

    Written permission

    HHS says your health information cannot be used or shared without your written permission unless the law allows it. For example, a provider generally cannot use or share it for marketing or advertising purposes without your authorization. That is why we plan to ask a patient for permission before any story is shown. [TBD: confirm with client how permission is collected]

  2. 02

    Your rights

    HHS lists your right to ask to see and get a copy of your health records, and to have corrections added. It also says you can ask your provider questions about your rights, and you are welcome to ask us.

  3. 03

    When you share your experience

    Please leave out personal health details. Please do not send medical details through this website.

Before You Decide

What to Look for When Reading Reviews of Any Dentist

If you are looking for a dentist in Oxnard, reviews can be one part of your research. The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) offers general tips for evaluating online reviews.

  1. Tip 01
    Use more than one source

    The FTC advises looking at a variety of sources, not just one site.

  2. Tip 02
    Check how recent

    The FTC says to check how recent reviews are and to watch for a burst of reviews over a short period of time, which can sometimes mean the reviews are fake.

  3. Tip 03
    Look at the reviewer

    The FTC suggests checking whether the reviewer has written other reviews. An account created to write one review may be fake.

  4. Tip 04
    A quick look is not enough

    The FTC says you cannot assume you can tell a real review from a fake one just by looking. It adds that fake reviews are not always positive.

  5. Tip 05
    A rating is only a number

    The FTC notes that some fake positive reviews give less than the highest possible rating to seem more believable.

  6. Tip 06
    Visit and decide for yourself

    MouthHealthy says that during your first visit you should be able to determine if a dentist is the right one for you.
