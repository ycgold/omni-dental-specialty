Your First Visit

What to Bring to Your First Visit

These general points can help you prepare for a first visit to our Oxnard office. Your dentist explains what applies to you. You can also read about our Oxnard dental office before you come.

What to bring

  • A full list of your current medications, including prescriptions, over-the-counter medicines and supplements
  • A completed medical history form, if one was requested
  • Your questions, written down
  • Something to take notes with, if that helps you remember advice
  • Anything else we ask for: [TBD: confirm with client what new patients should bring, such as ID or insurance information]

Cleveland Clinic lists a medication list and a medical history form among the things to bring to an appointment. The American Dental Association's MouthHealthy site suggests asking questions and taking notes.

What a checkup can include

  • Questions about your recent medical history
  • An exam of your mouth
  • A check of your gums, which the hygienist may do with special instruments
  • A decision about whether you need X-rays
  • Time to tell your dentist about any change in your overall health
  • Time to ask your dentist to explain anything step by step

MouthHealthy says the dentist or hygienist asks about your recent medical history and examines your mouth, decides whether you need X-rays, and may check your gums for gum disease. This describes a general checkup. Your visit depends on your needs. Please do not send medical details through this website.
