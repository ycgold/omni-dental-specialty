Everyday Care

General and Preventive

Routine visits, plus evaluation and care for concerns with your teeth, gums and jaw.

  • General Dentistry

    Routine exams, cleanings and patient education.

  • Gum Disease Treatment

    Evaluation and care for the health of your gums.

  • Emergency Dentistry

    Call the office to describe an urgent dental concern.

  • Root Canal

    Treatment for the inside of a tooth, planned after an exam.

  • TMJ Therapy

    Evaluation and options for jaw joint discomfort.

  • Wisdom Teeth

    Consultation about your third molars.

Smile and Restoration

Cosmetic and Restorative

Treatments that focus on how your teeth look, and options for rebuilding or replacing teeth.

  • Cosmetic Dentistry

    Treatments focused on the appearance of your teeth and gums.

  • Veneers

    Thin shells placed over the front surface of teeth.

  • Teeth Whitening

    Options for lightening tooth color. Individual results vary.

  • Invisalign

    Clear aligners used to move teeth gradually.

  • Dental Crowns

    Custom caps placed over a tooth, planned at an exam.

  • Dental Implants

    A tooth replacement option anchored in the jawbone.

What to Expect

What to Expect at a First Visit

We explain each stage so you know what happens before you arrive.

  1. Step 01
    Call or Request a Visit

    Call (805) 366-6000 or send a request. We confirm your appointment time with you.

  2. Step 02
    History and Exam

    We review your dental and health history, then complete an exam of your teeth and gums.

  3. Step 03
    Your Options Explained

    We explain what we find in plain language and talk through your options.

  4. Step 04
    Next Steps

    If further care is needed, we outline a plan and schedule your next visit. You decide what comes next.
