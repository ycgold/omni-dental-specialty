In Plain Language

What Dental Services in Oxnard Mean

These short notes explain common terms from the lists above. They are general information, not advice for your situation. Two of them have their own pages: dental implants and general dentistry.

01
Gum Disease

The National Institute of Dental and Craniofacial Research (NIDCR) describes gum disease as an infection that causes inflammation of the tissues that hold your teeth in place. It says swollen, red or bleeding gums are among the signs, and that a dentist or dental hygienist performs tests to diagnose it.

02
Root Canal

MouthHealthy says root canals are used to repair and save a tooth instead of removing it. It lists a deep cavity, a cracked tooth and an injury to the tooth among the causes of an infected pulp.

03
TMJ

NIDCR describes temporomandibular disorders as conditions that cause pain and dysfunction in the jaw joint. It says there is no widely accepted standard test to diagnose them, so an evaluation starts with your history and an exam.

04
Wisdom Teeth

MouthHealthy says wisdom teeth are your third molars, and that problems can come up when there is not enough space for them to surface. Before any decision, your dentist examines your mouth and takes an X-ray.

05
Whitening and Veneers

MouthHealthy says it is important to talk to your dentist before deciding to whiten your teeth. It describes veneers as custom made coverings for the front of teeth and notes that the treatment is not reversible.

06
Crowns and Aligners

MouthHealthy describes a crown as a cover that helps restore a tooth to its normal shape and size. It says orthodontic treatment corrects bad bites, and that removable aligners are used under direct professional supervision.

Before You Choose

Questions to Ask Before You Choose a Treatment

Bring this list to your visit and add your own. If you want to ask something before you come to our Oxnard office, contact us.

About the treatment

  • What is this treatment for, and what does it involve?
  • Are there other options, and what happens if I wait?
  • Does anything else need attention first, such as a cavity or my gums?
  • How will you check on the area after treatment?

Please do not send medical details through this website.

About planning and cost

  • What are the steps, and how many visits should I plan for?
  • What will each stage cost, and will I see the details before treatment is scheduled?
  • Does my insurance plan cover this treatment?
