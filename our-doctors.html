Our Approach

How We Approach Care in Oxnard

We listen, we explain, and you decide what comes next. Here is what that looks like when you visit our Oxnard office.

01
We Listen First

Your visit starts with your concerns. We want to hear what you have noticed and what you would like to ask.

02
We Explain in Plain Language

Your dentist explains what they find in words that make sense to you. If a term is unfamiliar, ask and we will explain it.

03
Your Questions Matter

MouthHealthy, the American Dental Association's patient site, encourages you to take time to ask questions and take notes if that will help you remember your dentist's advice.

04
Options Before Treatment

If your dentist suggests further care, we go over the options with you before anything is scheduled.

05
You Decide

Your dentist gives you the information, and you decide what comes next. You can take your time with that decision.

06
Your Health History Matters

Tell us about changes in your health or your medicines. MouthHealthy says it is important to keep your dentist informed of any changes in your overall health.

Your First Conversation

What Meeting Your Dentist in Oxnard Is Like

A first visit is mostly a conversation. These general points can help you feel ready when you come to our Oxnard office.

Things to tell your dentist

  • Your health history and any recent changes
  • The medicines you take
  • Anything you have noticed in your mouth
  • If you are pregnant or think you may be
  • If visiting the dentist makes you nervous

MedlinePlus says to tell your dentist if you are pregnant or think you may be, and to tell your dentist before your visit if you feel anxious. To ask us something before you come, contact us.

How a first conversation can go

  1. 1

    We listen to what brought you in

    We start with your concerns and the questions you would like answered.

  2. 2

    Your health history

    MouthHealthy says the dentist or hygienist asks about your recent medical history.

  3. 3

    A look at your teeth and gums

    MedlinePlus describes a typical dental exam as a cleaning followed by the dentist examining your mouth. Your visit may be arranged a little differently.

  4. 4

    Questions and next steps

    If further care comes up, we explain your options, and you decide what comes next.

Before You Choose

Questions You Can Ask Any Dentist

Use these general questions at any dental office and add your own. We are glad to answer them at our Oxnard office.

  1. 01

    Will you explain things step by step?

    MouthHealthy suggests asking your dentist to explain step by step. You can also ask what each part of a visit is for.

  2. 02

    How can I prevent problems?

    The MouthHealthy guide to finding a dentist suggests asking whether the dentist will explain ways to help you prevent dental health problems.

  3. 03

    How often should I come back?

    Cleveland Clinic suggests asking your dentist how often is right for you. MedlinePlus adds that you can ask how often you need X-rays.

  4. 04

    What are my options?

    If your dentist suggests further care, you can ask what the options are and what each one involves. We go over this with you before anything is scheduled.

  5. 05

    How are costs handled?
