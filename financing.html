Before You Decide

Questions to Ask About the Cost of Dental Care

It helps to understand a treatment plan and what it may cost before care begins. These are questions you can ask any dental office, including Omni Dental Specialty in Oxnard.

About the treatment plan

  • What does the treatment plan include, and in what order?
  • Which parts are estimates, and what could change them?
  • Can you explain each step so I understand it?
  • Are there other options I can talk through with the dentist?
  • Can I take the plan home in writing?

MouthHealthy suggests asking your dentist to explain step by step, and taking notes if that helps you remember your dentist's advice.

About paying and coverage

  • What may my dental plan cover, and what would I pay myself?
  • Will the office send the plan to my insurance company before treatment?
  • How does payment work, and when is it due?
  • Are fees and payment arrangements explained before treatment is scheduled?
  • Who can I call if I have a question about a bill?

Overview

Understanding Dental
Insurance in General Terms

Dental plans differ, and nothing on this page says which plans our Oxnard office accepts. [TBD: confirm with client, accepted insurance plans] The general terms below can help you read your own plan.

MouthHealthy explains that an annual limit is the total dollar amount a dental plan will pay during the plan year, and that a deductible is the amount you are expected to pay out of pocket before your plan begins covering your expenses. It describes coinsurance as the split that spells out what percentage of dental costs your plan will pay, and exclusions as services your plan does not cover.

Cleveland Clinic describes dental insurance as a contract between you and the plan you choose. MouthHealthy also explains in its overview of plan types that a dental PPO lets you choose any dental professional in the plan's provider network, while discount plans are not insurance because they do not pay dental expenses for you.

Your plan documents or your plan's member line can tell you how your own coverage works. You can read about our Oxnard dental office before you call us with questions.
