What We Offer

Dental Services
in Oxnard

General and cosmetic dental care for adults and families. We offer services under one roof at 1690 E. Gonzales Road.

Dental Implants

Tooth replacement anchored in the jawbone, designed to look and feel like natural teeth.

Veneers

Thin porcelain shells bonded to the front surface of teeth to address chips, discoloration, and minor shape concerns.

Invisalign

Clear aligner therapy for gradual tooth movement without traditional metal braces.

General Dentistry

Routine exams, cleanings, fillings, and preventive care to keep your teeth healthy at every stage of life.

Cosmetic Dentistry

A range of treatments focused on the appearance of your teeth and gums, tailored to your goals.

Teeth Whitening

Teeth whitening options to lighten tooth color. Results vary by individual.

Gum Disease Treatment

Evaluation and care for gum health at various stages, including scaling and root planing.

Root Canal

Treatment to remove infected tissue from inside a tooth and preserve the root structure when possible.

Emergency Dentistry

Care for urgent dental concerns such as pain, a broken tooth, or swelling. Call us to describe your situation.

TMJ Therapy

Evaluation and management options for jaw joint discomfort. We discuss your symptoms and available approaches.

Wisdom Teeth

Consultation and removal of third molars when they cause crowding, impaction, or discomfort.

Dental Crowns

Custom-fitted caps placed over a damaged or weakened tooth to restore its shape, size, and function.

Your Visit, Step by Step

We keep the process clear so you know what happens at each stage before you arrive.

Step 01
Call or Book Online

Contact us at (805) 366-6000 or use the request form below. A member of our team will confirm your appointment and answer any questions before you arrive.

Step 02
Exam and Records

On your first visit we review your dental history, take records, and conduct a thorough exam. We want a complete picture before recommending anything.

Step 03
Your Plan Explained

We walk you through our findings in plain language. You decide what to move forward with and when. You decide what comes next.

Step 04
Ongoing Care

We schedule follow-up visits based on your needs and remain available to answer questions throughout your care.
