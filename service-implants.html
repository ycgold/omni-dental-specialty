Overview

What Is a
Dental Implant?

A dental implant is a small titanium post placed into the jawbone to replace the root of a missing tooth. Once the area has healed, a custom crown is attached on top, creating a restoration that functions and looks similar to a natural tooth.

Implants are one option among several for replacing a missing tooth. During your consultation at Omni Dental Specialty, our dentist will review your dental history and discuss which approach fits your situation.

The Process

How Implants Work, Step by Step

We walk you through each stage before any treatment begins so you know what to expect.

Step 01
Consultation and Exam

We review your dental and health history, take records, and assess whether implants are an appropriate option for your situation.

Step 02
Treatment Planning

If implants are a fit, we put together a plan and explain the timeline, what each appointment involves, and what to expect between visits.

Step 03
Implant Placement

The titanium post is placed into the jawbone. A healing period follows during which the implant integrates with the surrounding bone tissue.

Step 04
Crown Placement

Once healing is complete, a custom crown is attached to the implant post. We check fit, bite, and appearance before you leave.

Good to Know

A Closer Look at Implant Care

These general points can help you prepare for a consultation. Your dentist explains what applies to your situation.

  1. 01

    What is part of an implant restoration

    An implant restoration usually has three parts. The implant sits in the jaw, a connector piece joins it to the crown, and the crown is the part you see. The exact parts can vary, so your dentist explains what applies to you.

  2. 02

    What your dentist reviews first

    Your visit usually starts with your health history and a look at your mouth. Imaging may be taken as needed. You also have time to talk about your goals and ask questions.

  3. 03

    Looking after your teeth around an implant

    Daily brushing and flossing and regular dental visits are part of ongoing care. Tell your dentist if you notice any changes in the area. Your dentist explains the care that fits you.

  4. 04

    Questions to bring with you

    Write down what you want to know before you come in. You might ask what each step involves, how many visits to expect and how to care for the area. Your dentist answers in plain language.

What to Consider

Why Patients Ask About Implants

01
Placed in the Jawbone

The titanium post is placed into the jawbone and the bone bonds with it over time. The crown is attached to the post, not to the neighboring teeth.

02
Custom Crown

The crown is made to match the shade and shape of the surrounding teeth. Your dentist discusses the details at the consultation.

03
Ongoing Care

Daily brushing, flossing and regular dental visits are part of caring for an implant. Your dentist explains what ongoing care looks like for you.

Candidacy depends on factors including jawbone density, gum health, and overall medical history. The only way to know is through a clinical evaluation. We review these details at your consultation and explain what is involved before recommending anything.
The timeline varies by individual. After the implant post is placed, a healing period is needed before the crown can be attached. We outline the expected timeline during treatment planning so you know what to plan for.
The placement appointment is performed with local anesthesia. Some patients experience soreness in the days following. We discuss what to expect and provide aftercare instructions at each stage.
Coverage varies widely by plan. Some plans cover a portion of the restoration; many do not cover the implant post itself. We recommend contacting your insurance provider directly to understand your benefits before scheduling.
Implants are cleaned the same way as natural teeth: brushing twice daily and flossing. Regular dental visits allow us to monitor the implant and surrounding tissue. We review specific care instructions at your later appointments.
