About the Blog

What Our Blog Covers
for Oxnard Patients

Our blog explains dental topics in plain language for patients in Oxnard and nearby communities. The articles are meant to help you understand your own questions before you talk with your dentist.

We welcome patients from Oxnard, El Rio and College Park, and the topics come from questions people ask about their teeth and gums. The articles are general information, so they cannot say what is right for your mouth. MedlinePlus says that after your dental exam, your dentist will discuss any oral health problems you may have and your treatment options.

You can visit Omni Dental Specialty in Oxnard to see what we do, or read about our Oxnard dental office.
